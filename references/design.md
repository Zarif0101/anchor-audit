# Design module (draft, untested)

Slot = 1 reference he likes + 1 stated dislike. Build toward the reference and away from the dislike before anything else.

## AI-look tells (audit only)
- Purple-to-blue or teal gradients, glassmorphism, glowing blobs.
- A centered hero with a vague headline ("Unlock your potential") and two pill buttons.
- Three equal cards with an icon, a title and two lines each.
- Emoji or generic line icons doing the work of content.
- One sans font everywhere, every corner rounded the same, soft shadow on everything.
- Stock 3D renders, abstract waves, people pointing at laptops.

## Moves
- Make one asymmetric decision on purpose: a big number, an off-center image, or a tight crop.
- Use a real asset of his (photo, screenshot, handwriting) over any generated one.
- Take the palette from the reference, max 3 colors plus neutrals.
- Pick one typeface with character for display and keep body plain.
- Use density where the content is dense. White space is not a style by itself.
- Write real copy into the layout. Lorem ipsum hides weak structure.
