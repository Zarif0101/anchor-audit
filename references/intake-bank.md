# Intake bank

Pick up to 3 per piece and mix them differently each time. "None" is always an allowed answer.

## Any medium
1. What number is in this? (a count, a price, a time, a score)
2. Where did this happen, or where are you when you think about it?
3. What do most people get wrong about this?
4. What did you try that didn't work?
5. Who said something about this that stuck with you? What exactly did they say?
6. What would you bet on here, and what would make you lose the bet?
7. What's the one thing you'd cut if this had to be half as long?
8. Who is this for, specifically? One real person is fine.
9. What do you refuse to say in this piece, even though everyone else says it?
10. What happened right before you decided to make this?

## Design
- Send 1 reference you like. Name 1 thing you dislike in designs like this.

## Video
- Say or type the first line you'd actually say on camera.

## Code
- What are the real names (project, data, users)? What constraint is non-negotiable?
