# Audit pass (patch-only)

Run once per piece, on the whole draft, after drafting. Output = edited sentences only, applied in place.

## Procedure
1. Read `tells.md`. Scan the draft category by category.
2. For each hit, rewrite only that sentence using a move from the bank below. Skip anything inside «».
3. Check `last-moves.md`. Do not reuse an opener shape or a move that appears in the last 10 entries. Pick a different one.
4. Specificity check: find the single most generic sentence left. Replace it with a concrete fact from the brief or intake. If no fact exists, cut the sentence.
5. Length check: cut 10-20% if any paragraph restates the one before it.
6. Log the opener shape and the 1-3 moves used to `last-moves.md`.

Do not rewrite sentences that pass. Do not add flourishes to replace removed ones. A shorter piece is a fine result.

## Move bank (rotate; never the same lead move twice in a row)
- M1 Swap the abstract noun for the thing itself ("growth" → "312 new subscribers in June").
- M2 Split a compound sentence; drop the connector.
- M3 Turn a list of three into the one item that matters, said fully.
- M4 Replace a rhetorical question with the answer as a flat statement.
- M5 Replace a hedge stack with one honest qualifier, or none.
- M6 Move the conclusion to the first sentence.
- M7 Replace a general claim with a small scene: who, where, what was said.
- M8 Use the reader's own words, the ones they'd type into a search box.
- M9 Delete the sentence. Check that nothing is lost.
- M10 Add the counter-view in one line, then say why you still disagree.

## Opener shapes (log which one was used)
number-first · scene-first · claim-first · quote-first · question-he-actually-got · confession · mid-action
